# AppPage.tsx

The bar shows whether the app answers and its unit's state, with Start when it is stopped, Restart and Stop when it runs, and a link to open the app in its own tab. The frame is reloaded after an action, so a restarted app shows its fresh page.

Apps are listed at the end of the sidebar, under Apps, with a dot that is green when the app answered at the last check (every 30 seconds).
