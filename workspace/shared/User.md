# User.md: about the person this agent trades for

## Who the user is

- Name: Pramod
- Experience with trading: 5 years, has traded all segments.
- Experience with AI agents: none; first time using AI agents or automated trading.
- What he wants: the agent to run his strategies autonomously.

## Money and risk

All amounts are percentages of the total account balance across all brokers, read live from UBI, not fixed rupee amounts.

- Capital the agent may use: 100% of the total balance.
- Largest loss acceptable in one day: 15% of the total balance.
- Largest loss acceptable in one week: 35% of the total balance.
- Largest single order: 25% of the total balance.
- Open positions: no cap.
- Capital split between desks: one shared pool; any desk may use any part of it, limited only by the account-wide limits.
- Pramod chose these numbers knowingly after being told they are aggressive.

- Markets and segments: NSE equity cash, NSE F&O, BSE, MCX commodities, currency.
- Instruments to focus on or avoid: all instruments are allowed; nothing is excluded.
- Options: long positions only. No option writing without his explicit permission.
- Preferred holding periods: intraday, overnight and multi-day are all allowed.
- Desks: "main" is general-purpose for now; intraday, scalper and positional desks will be created in a later milestone.

## Schedule and contact

- Before the market opens: a short plan.
- During the day: a message for every order and fill; alerts on important events such as a stop hit, a risk-engine refusal, or nearing the daily loss limit.
- End of day: summary of P&L and open positions.
- Weekly: a review.
- Urgent, at any time: daily or weekly loss limit hit, broker or UBI connection problem, a position left open when it should not be.
- Quiet hours: none.
